# Project Charter

This project develops a reproducible Management Strategy Evaluation for the North-Central stock of Peruvian anchovy (*Engraulis ringens*). The workflow will progressively document management objectives, stock ecology, fishery dynamics, management, monitoring, stock assessment, operating models, observation and estimation, candidate management procedures, implementation error, performance metrics, uncertainty sets, closed-loop evaluation, exceptional circumstances, and post-adoption review.

Scientific lead: IMARPE, DIPEL. Management authority: PRODUCE.
